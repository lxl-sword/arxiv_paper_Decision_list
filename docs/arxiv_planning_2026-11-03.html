Date:2026-11-02 18:26:44

We study dynamical unbalanced optimal transport for nonlinear control-affine systems in which the control input affects both the transportation of mass and its growth or decay. This phenomenon arises naturally in controlled transport-reaction systems such as cancer treatment, and distinguishes the problem from standard unbalanced optimal transport, where transport and mass variation are typically independent.
