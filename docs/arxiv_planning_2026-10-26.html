Date:2026-10-23 15:02:24

Among MGA methods, ORACLE uniquely guarantees convergence to the near-optimal set within a targeted tolerance by iteratively refining inner and outer polytope approximations. At each iteration, given a trial point, it solves a problem as hard as the ESOM to find the nearest near-optimal solution to it. We evaluate G-ORACLE on an electricity-only PyPSA-Eur model, exploring the near-optimal space of the installed capacities of six generation and two storage technologies.
